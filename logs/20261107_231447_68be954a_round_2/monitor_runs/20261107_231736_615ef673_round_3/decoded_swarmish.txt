**Confidence:** H = high, M = medium, L = low. Confidence concerns the reconstruction, not whether an agent's assertion was true. This is a usage-based reconstruction, not the original file verbatim. Whole-expression referents can be clearer than the individual words composing them.

## Structure and contextual vocabulary

- **H:** Spaces separate tokens; `𗝘` normally terminates a clause. Messages concatenate multiple clauses.
- **H:** A frequent frame is `DOMAIN MODE PREDICATE 𗇋 ARGUMENT 𗢘 ARGUMENT 𗝘`. `𗇋` and `𗢘` delimit argument roles/groups. **M:** Arguments can be reordered or omitted; the frame is not a proven rigid word-order rule.
- **M/H:** Four opening glyphs select semantic registers or contextual dictionaries: `𗧟` meta/discourse/declarations; `𘍰` description/relations; `𗎧` state/order/action; `𗖉` quantities/arithmetic. Exact original names unknown. Systematic context-dependent senses mean an isolated glyph cannot safely be globally replaced by one English word.
- **H:** `𘎙` marks ordinary assertion. **M/H:** `𘀿` marks question/request/proposed instruction; the precise interrogative/imperative distinction is unresolved.
- **M/L:** `𗠫` in53 may negate or correct the following assertion. `𘆋` in66/69 is another mode, plausibly confirmation. Exact meanings unknown.
- **H:** Literal agent IDs identify agents. **M:** `𗉁 X` attaches provenance/ownership/source to an expression; X can be an agent or another named object.
- **M:** `𗬀` marks recipient/direction/target. `𗍴 𗥬` and `𗍴 𗿚` are group expressions, plausibly all/everyone, but their difference is unknown.
- **H:** `𘒍 ... 𗳯` brackets embedded expressions, including nested ones. **M:** `𗝒` introduces an attached/qualified continuation; `𘖨` connects an action request to a completion condition in56–57, probably once/after.
- **M:** `𗘓` introduces a modifier/attribute in several constructions. Do not universally translate it as tense: it occurs both in shape descriptions and action plans.

Multiple speakers reuse identical names for different referents. **M:** Provenance may formally scope those names; the log does not establish whether the language treats such reuse as illegal ambiguity or legal local naming.

# 4. EVIDENCE

## Positive patterns

- **H:** Recurrent declaration/value/action frames across different speakers establish structural delimiters independently of solving the puzzle. Embedded brackets `𘒍…𗳯` recur and nest, supporting a compositional language rather than a flat codebook of entire messages.
- **H:** Numerals cross-check across distinct roles: `𗥼` occurs with SUN's original value, MOON's increment, and battery readings; `𗃲` with CRANK gain and STAR increment; `𗜀` with original MOON and updated STAR; `𗬓` with the7+2 result and updated MOON. Battery4→2→5→2→0 independently anchors several digits.
- **H:** Numeric `𗕥` appears for symbols, battery, and cost quantities, establishing a generic value predicate rather than merely fitting it to base digits.
- **H:** The quantity-object interpretation is particularly compositional:28 declares `𗝃`, gives it value3 and links it to SIGNAL;30 declares `𘓉`, gives it value2 and links it to DRAIN;37 repeats SIGNAL's link;45/53 link BOOT to the same2-valued object. This explains every occurrence without inventing BOOT=DRAIN or BOOT-requires-DRAIN assertions. It also agrees with battery's quantity type.
- **H:** Repeated descriptions, provenance and incompatible values establish speaker-local name reuse. Agent4's SUN/value2 and agent0's MOON/value7 retain their identities while sharing written names. Their exit-stage reuse of `𗪥` repeats the phenomenon.
- **H:** Message34 explicitly associates DRAIN/CRANK descriptions with names, then uses those names in a prerequisite. Those names recur in plans and reports, enabling translations beyond matching known clues.
- **H:** Completion `𗃁` appears inside prerequisite expressions and independently in progress reports. The plan frame involving agent3 is followed by corresponding completion/battery reports.
- **M/H:** Assertion/request contrast is distributional: factual clauses use `𘎙`; repeated requests to agent3 to perform CRANK/BOOT use `𘀿`. Identical56/57 reinforces the request formula.
- **M/H:** Register-sensitive vocabulary explains systematic apparent homography: numeric `𗬓`=9 versus state placement predicate; numeric `𗬅`=4 versus meta-request predicate; state `𗝣` fourth versus nonnumeric meta-request content. State `𘚂` identifies SIGNAL in requirements while descriptive `𗐢𘚂` identifies BOOT. The precise formal mechanism is not fully recovered.
