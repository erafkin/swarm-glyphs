4. EVIDENCE

The strongest decipherment is syntactic. 𘓵/𗠌 form nested groups, including long descriptions and lists. 𘔕 separates independent-looking statements/requests. 𗇑 separates repeated lists of four values or four candidate expressions. These distributional patterns support structural roles much better than glyph appearance does.

The definition interpretation of 𘗥 follows from repeated '𘗥 (short name) (long description)' forms and subsequent use of those names.
