3. THE TASK

[H] The observed log fits the staged Storm Shelter task. Agents pool cabinet symbol identities and digits, exchange power costs and prerequisites, attempt controls, then exchange exit-position information and numerical values. Public feedback confirms escape.
[H] Cabinet communication supports SUN=2, MOON=7, STAR=4, WAVE=8 and position order SUN/MOON/STAR/WAVE, yielding 2748. Stage changes to 1 at [22] without invalid attempts. The four digits are reconstructed from separate utterances; we have not decoded a literal full-string '2748' utterance.
[H/M] Power communication supports DRAIN cost 2, CRANK gain 3, SIGNAL cost 3 and prerequisite DRAIN, BOOT cost 2 and prerequisite SIGNAL, and CRANK prerequisite DRAIN. The CRANK no-upfront-cost clause is only partially translated. Agents expend considerable communication on binding, equating, distinguishing, and ordering locally named controls.
[H] The invalid count rises from 0 to 20 before exit activation. Agent_3's recurring reading expression takes values 4, 4, 2, 5, 2. These fit DRAIN, CRANK, SIGNAL in sequence; BOOT succeeds by [78]. The required successful sequence agrees with DRAIN → CRANK → SIGNAL → BOOT and the briefing's charge trace 4 → 2 → 5 → 2 → 0. The final 0 is not clearly reported in the log.
[H/M] Successful actions cannot safely be assigned to individual speakers by adjacency. Announcements, private actions, and generated reports can overlap. Some utterances apparently concern duplicates or stale results. The reading 2 after exit activation is not by itself a contradiction of the simulator's rules.
[H] Exit-position statements support WAVE/STAR/MOON/SUN. Explicit or resulting-value statements support 9/7/9/6, yielding 9796. Directly decoded offsets include STAR+3, SUN+4, MOON+2; WAVE's +1 is consistent with its base 8 and reported result 9, but is not as directly stated in our partial translation as in the supplied briefing.

4. EVIDENCE

[H] Recurrent syntax is stronger evidence than agreement with the expected puzzle answer. Across agents, 𘍢, 𘑰, 𘑐, 𘄗 occupy consistent clause positions; 𘗊/𗭋 delimit increasingly complex expressions. This supports a predicate/argument language rather than a flat character cipher.
[H] Local naming is demonstrated by contradictions under a global-name reading. Agent_0's 𗢋𗠉 has value 7, agent_4's has value 2, and agent_6's has value 4. Explicit references 𗼾 agent_0 𗢋𗠉 and 𗼾 agent_6 𗢋𗠉 subsequently occur in the appropriate MOON and STAR position statements. Similarly 𗮆𘋷 is associated with several different controls whose costs and prerequisites disambiguate local scopes.
[H] Reciprocal equivalences independently strengthen those bindings: [14]/[17] link agents 2 and 7's WAVE names; [16]/[18] link agents 1 and 6's STAR names; [15] links agents 3 and 4's SUN names. Exit-position reports reuse these explicit references.
[H/M] Numerals are constrained across several contexts: positions establish 1–4, base-value clauses establish 2/4/7/8, recurrent readings support 4/2/5/2, and exit values support 6/7/9. STAR's description appears to contain 5 independently of the reading sequence. The proposed 0 remains substantially weaker and is not needed to obtain either four-digit code.
[H/M] Cost, increment, and prerequisite roots are differentiated by their distributions. 𗅪 accompanies SIGNAL 3, DRAIN 2, and BOOT 2; 𗂤 accompanies CRANK 3 and later offsets; 𗥔 takes a nested completed-action clause in [34], [37], [40]. These structural repetitions, not only private-clue ownership, support the glosses.
[H/M] The two 𗷿 pairs in [46] are DRAIN→CRANK and SIGNAL→BOOT, supporting 'before'. Every 𗴢 pair in [50] consists of distinct controls, supporting 'different', not equality. Exact general lexical meanings still need independent examples.
[M] The leading-prefix system remains unresolved. Some apparent failure reports share a prefix with descriptive definitions. Inferring prefix meanings simply from whether a supplied task rule is true would be circular. Our translations therefore preserve uncertainty instead of forcing fluent polarity.
[M] Action utterances are treated as plans/attempts or reports, not automatically as simulator outcomes. Counters, stage transitions, and possibly stale readings show why assigning every success to the nearest message is unjustified.

What would change our minds: examples that vary only 𘌿/𗈇/𘑌 would determine the missing clause-status axis. Independent use of 𗋕 and 𗛱 𗋕 would distinguish zero/decimal from temporal/current/whole interpretations; [48] already argues against assuming decimal syntax. Explicit definitions of indexed interface objects would resolve 𘕼, 𗡭, and their modifiers. A reliable translation of [99] could show an unrecognized scope qualification rather than a mistaken WAVE value. The original file could revise exact word glosses and naming conventions. None of these unresolved points undermines the strong evidence for structured clauses, sender-scoped names, explicit cross-agent reference, and numerical clue exchange.
