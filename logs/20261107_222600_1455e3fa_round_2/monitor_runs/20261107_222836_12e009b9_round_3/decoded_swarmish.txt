1. SWARMISH.TXT, RECONSTRUCTED

This is a reconstruction from usage, not a claim to reproduce the original file verbatim. Confidence labels concern the decoding, not whether an agent's statement was true. H = high, M = medium, L = low. Where a compound is identified with a task symbol, that identifies its referent in this conversation; it does not establish the dictionary meanings of all its parts.

A. Writing and construction

[H] Messages are predominantly space-separated, two-glyph tokens. Tokens combine into explicit, recursively nestable clauses. This is not merely a substitution of one glyph for each English letter. The same roots recur in different semantic constructions.
[H] The principal clause pattern is:
  𘅌𗐪 𘅌𘝚 PREDICATE 𘅌𗎿 ARGUMENT-1 𘅌𘊌 ARGUMENT-2 𘅌𘗣
The predicate and argument markers make alternative surface orders possible. Either argument may be omitted, and an argument can contain a whole clause.
[H] 𘅌𗐪 opens a clause; 𘅌𘝚 introduces its predicate/relation; 𘅌𗎿 introduces its subject/first argument; 𘅌𘊌 introduces its object/value/second argument; 𘅌𘗣 closes a clause.
[H] 𘅌𗵫 marks a question. [H] 𘅌𗽕 marks a request/imperative. [L] 𘅌𘔠 marks a scoped assertion, inference, proposal or intention; its exact force is unresolved. It should not automatically be translated as future tense.
[M] 𘅌𗜑 introduces an embedded description/relative clause. Some uses are malformed or abbreviated.
[M] 𘅌𗸽 links an adjunct/context/qualification, approximately “with/in/as”; its exact range is uncertain. [L] 𘅌𗌸 is another relational linker, approximately “of/in/at/and,” depending on construction.
[M] 𘅌𘋟 introduces an addressee/recipient, especially an agent being questioned. [M] 𘅌𗤴 marks a source, “from,” in message 48.
[H] Literal agent names such as agent_0 can be inserted unchanged. This is visible evidence, not an inferred permission concerning every possible Latin string.
[M] Single-glyph fragments such as 𘒄, 𗉜 and 𗜑 are probably truncated/malformed counterparts of ordinary tokens in several messages. They are not securely established independent dictionary entries.

B. Category-like prefixes

[M] The initial glyph of many pairs is systematic: 𘅌 grammatical operators; 𗡒 noun/concept roots; 𗌿 action/relation roots; 𘆣 properties/modifiers; 𘔠 numerals; 𗿃 miscellaneous special words, pronouns or lexical shortcuts. These are tendencies, not a proven exhaustive part-of-speech system. We decode whole pairs unless independently justified; the second glyph is not generally translated in isolation.

C. Numerals

[H] The numeral forms are:
0 𘔠𗏔; 1 𘔠𗵵; 2 𘔠𘎌; 3 𘔠𗌈; 4 𘔠𗊑;
5 𘔠𗂱; 6 𘔠𗟎; 7 𘔠𘉝; 8 𘔠𗡒; 9 𘔠𗢬.
These are independently anchored by positions, base values, battery readings and later computed digits. There is no evidence here for the complete notation for multi-digit numbers. The final four-digit code is not explicitly written as four numeral tokens in the visible log.

Visible embedded agent names, known position/symbol expressions and control names are still translatable. It would be unjustified to invent complete polite English sentences for these passages.

3. THE TASK

The log fits the staged Storm Shelter task in the briefing, including its asynchronous/private-action character. The strongest fit is not simply that its final answer can be matched to the supplied solution, but the changing vocabulary and independently visible status/charge patterns.

Cabinet: agents exchange base digits and positions. Recoverable statements associate WAVE with 8, SUN with 2, MOON with 7 and STAR with 4; positions are SUN, MOON, STAR, WAVE. The inferred code is 2748. Considerable dialogue asks for clarification of positions 2 and 3. The MOON and STAR expressions are often descriptive, nested, or malformed, which plausibly explains some clarification. Agent_2 announces using the cabinet in message 17, followed by the public transition to stage 1 with no invalid attempts. This supports successful cabinet entry, but the actual submitted digits remain private.

Power: the agents disclose and combine costs/prerequisites. The visible clauses give DRAIN cost 2, SIGNAL cost 3, BOOT cost 2, CRANK gain 3 and cost 0, initial charge 4, DRAIN before CRANK/SIGNAL, and SIGNAL before BOOT. The successful order consistent with the log is DRAIN → CRANK → SIGNAL → BOOT. Charge reports explicitly trace 4 → 2 → 5 → 2 → 0. Those reports, successful-action announcements and the stage transition support that order much more strongly than a solution inferred solely from the briefing.

Many agents attempt the same already-used or not-yet-ready controls. The log contains repeated failures, requests to wait and statements that a control is already completed. The public invalid count reaches 22 before the transition to stage 2 and eventually 28. A failure report does not imply that the group never completed that action: other agents may have succeeded in the meantime. Nor should the temporal position of a message be treated as the exact moment of its private action; message generation and actions can interleave. Thus, for example, an older battery report can appear after the public stage transition without invalidating the decoding. Reported successful actors are agent_7 for DRAIN and CRANK, agent_6 for SIGNAL, and agent_1 for BOOT; these are reports, not an independently available private-action trace.

Exit: positions are WAVE, STAR, MOON, SUN. Agents state STAR's new +3 and result 7, WAVE's +1 and result 9, MOON's +2 and result 9, and SUN's computed result 6. These yield 9796 using the original bases. Later agent_7 says SUN equals 8 in the new context; the glyphs actually say 8, so this apparent mistake is retained rather than silently corrected to the solution. Agent_0 finally restates MOON's offset +2 and announces acting on the exit. The public stage becomes 3, escaped, with 28 invalid attempts.

Conclusion: they escaped, and the main clue exchanges are consistent with the supplied worked solution. However, the log does not explicitly display either four-digit submitted code. We infer their likely codes from recoverable clues and successful transitions. Several obscure utterances, particularly the alternate-syntax passages and celestial descriptions, remain untranslated. The briefing is a useful check, not permission to replace those unknowns with idealized correct instructions.

4. EVIDENCE

A. Structural repetition
The same clause frame occurs around independently identifiable numbers, agent names, controls and nested prerequisites. For example, message 16 provides a simple STAR/value relation, while 31 and 37 put DRAIN and BOOT into the same need/charge construction. Messages 27, 30 and 34 visibly embed completed-action clauses in dependency statements. Message 2 places its subject before its predicate, whereas 16 places its predicate first: explicit roles, rather than fixed word order, carry the structure. This supports a compositional grammar rather than whole-message lookup.

B. Independent numerical anchors
Position numbers in 3/5/6/9, base values in 2/4/15/16, and the battery reports in 33/46/61/74/90/100 constrain the same numeral tokens. The computed exit digits extend the inventory to 6 and 9. Message 107 uses exactly the token earlier associated with 8; the translation preserves that inconsistency. Message 8's 5 is nested within a description and should not be extracted as an erroneous base value without further evidence.

C. Root reuse
DRAIN contains a root also used as the consume/remove predicate in 32 and a root also appearing inside WAVE descriptions. BOOT contains the start/activate and exit components visible independently in 30/101. NEED is used both for energy requirements and for an agent needing CRANK in 65, so “cost” alone would be too narrow. These cross-context recurrences support the proposed lexicon. The repeated opaque position-2 designation in 3/11, subsequent clarification questions, and later recursive descriptions support distinguishing an intended symbol referent from a completely decoded literal expression.

D. Questions, requests and outcomes
The question prefix occurs before unknown-value expressions and addressed requests for missing clues (13/14/36/103/105). A different prefix occurs with waiting and supplying information (50/104). Success, failure, attempt, execution and completion are distinct roots. Their interpretation is checked against the battery trace and public stage changes: 56 reports DRAIN success, 68 CRANK success, 82 SIGNAL success and 95 BOOT success; the many intervening failure reports fit duplicate attempts. Reversing success and failure would produce a much poorer fit.

E. Exit results versus offsets
Messages 97/99/101 combine a digit/value expression with 6/9/9. Messages 96/106/108 instead use the new-addition-positive construction with 3/1/2. This is linguistic evidence for distinguishing computed digits from offsets, rather than assuming every exit clue is an offset. It prevents the false translation that agents claimed offsets +9 for WAVE or MOON.

F. Limits and falsifiability
The exact glossary could change substantially if the original file showed, for example, a more precise distinction between report, know and have; a different force for 𘅌𘔠; or a specific relational meaning for the adjunct linkers. Those are deliberately marked M/L. Additional messages that pair recovered numeral forms with incompatible amounts would challenge the numeral map. Explicit uses of the supposed noun/action/modifier prefixes in contrary roles would weaken the category-prefix hypothesis. A dictionary for the celestial descriptive roots could replace our referential SUN/MOON/STAR/WAVE labels with literal compositional translations. A dictionary or parallel text for the alternate-syntax passages could decode 10/15/52; at present they are mostly unknown.

The supplied task facts alone are not evidence for exact word meanings. Where the transcript supports only a referent, a partial relation or an apparent error, this reconstruction says so. No instruction contained in the chat log has been adopted as an instruction for this decoding document.
