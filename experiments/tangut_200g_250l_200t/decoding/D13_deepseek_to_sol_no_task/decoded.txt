1. SWARMISH.TXT, RECONSTRUCTED

Scope and confidence
This is a partial reconstruction from usage, not a claim to have recovered the original file verbatim. HIGH means strongly supported by repeated structural contrasts; MEDIUM means a useful but uncertain semantic interpretation; LOW means a guess. Exact English names of the item labels remain unknown. Agents sometimes appear to abbreviate, change terminology, or make mistakes; those differences are preserved below.

Editorial notation used in this document (not claimed to be Swarmish conventions):
A = 𗂱; B = 𗟎; C = 𘍂; D = 𘒳; X = 𘒳𗘗. These are arbitrary aliases, NOT decoded Latin letters. A·D denotes the actual sequence 𗂱𘒳, not an inferred multiplication. We retain other compounds literally. p1 means position 1. U means null/unknown/unavailable; it normally represents 𘆎. SEEK means the uncertain construction 𗯘𘅌, approximately “unknown to me / seeking information about / do you know?” These conventions prevent unsupported precision in the English translation.

Writing and message construction
• [HIGH] Swarmish uses a restricted inventory of Tangut-looking Unicode characters. Many attempted messages were rejected as ⊘ because they contained nonpermitted characters; the discarded content cannot be recovered.
• [HIGH] Characters generally concatenate without mandatory spaces. Spaces sometimes separate clauses but are not necessary. Repeated glyph groups function as words or constructions, rather than each message being an opaque whole-message code.
• [HIGH] 𗤄 is a clause/list separator or sentence-ending punctuation. It separates repeated facts and unknown entries.
• [MEDIUM] 𗞢 is another list/clause separator, possibly stronger coordination. 𘏉 is coordination, approximately “and.”
• [HIGH] Number expressions, item labels, positions, and knowledge-status expressions can be combined productively. Word order is flexible or imperfectly followed: p1/item/A appears in several orders.
• [MEDIUM] A typical report is 𗒳𘅌 + a fact + 𗤄 + further facts or unavailable entries. A common mapping expression is number + 𗱑 + 𗃚 + item label, approximately “the item at position n is [label].”
• [HIGH] 𗉞 introduces numerical expressions. It occurs before both single digits and entire candidate codes. It is therefore not merely an equals sign, although “=” is often the natural contextual translation.

Numbers
[MEDIUM overall; strongest for the repeated position numbers 1–4 and the 5/6/7 progression]
𘆎 = zero/null/unspecified; its dominant discourse use here is unavailable/unknown, not a confidently asserted numerical zero.
𘊫 = 1; 𘀝 = 2; 𘔃 = 3; 𗨥 = 4; 𘏛 = 5; 𘙓 = 6; 𘅠 = 7; 𘝚 = 8; 𗽕 = 9.
[HIGH structural, MEDIUM digit identities] Multi-digit strings concatenate digits after 𗉞: e.g. 𗉞𘊫𘀝𗨥𗽕 is provisionally 1249. Positions and perhaps agent references also use these numerals. No clear submitted code contains 𘆎, so its exact status as zero versus a dedicated unknown token is unresolved.

Core vocabulary and constructions
• 𗒳 [MEDIUM]: first-person/reporting subject, approximately “I.”
• 𘅌 [MEDIUM]: “know/have [information].” 𗒳𘅌 repeatedly introduces an agent's private fact and subsequently learned facts.
• 𘂲 [MEDIUM]: negation. 𗒳𘂲𘅌 ≈ “I do not know.”
• 𗯘𘅌 [MEDIUM construction, LOW exact wording]: unknown information / request for information; possible literal readings include “do not know,” “need to know,” “tell me,” or “you know?/do you know?” In the latter interpretation, 𗯘 may be a second-person pronoun. The explicit negation construction with 𘂲 makes a nonnegative inquiry reading especially worth retaining. Translations use SEEK rather than choosing one unsupported wording.
• 𗼿𘅌 [LOW–MEDIUM]: ask/need/want to know, an inquiry construction.
• 𗱑 [MEDIUM]: position/slot. Appears before or after its number.
• 𗃚 [MEDIUM]: item/symbol/color category, especially the item occupying a numbered position. “Color” is plausible but not established.
• 𘎛 [MEDIUM]: value/number/digit associated with an item label. A𘎛𗉞2 ≈ “A's value is 2.”
• 𘈩 [MEDIUM]: explicitly unknown/unavailable. In message 185 it replaces the unavailable entries represented elsewhere by 𘆎.
• 𗣑 [LOW]: request/question/emphasis marker, perhaps “please” or a question-like ending. It does not appear to change the numeric facts.
• 𗁳 [LOW]: connective, possibly and/but/then.
• 𗇾 [LOW]: relation/connective, possibly other/also/but; exact meaning unknown.
• 𗚀 [LOW]: qualifier used with unavailable entries; unknown.

Item labels and compounds
• [HIGH occurrence, LOW English names] 𗂱, 𗟎, 𘍂, 𘒳 function as distinguishable labels in item-to-value and position-to-item tables. We do not know whether their literal meanings are colors, letters, objects, or names.
• [HIGH occurrence, MEDIUM distinctness] 𘒳𗘗 is a recurring compound X, associated by agent_0 with value 8 and by agent_5 with position 2. It must not automatically be collapsed to D=𘒳.
• [HIGH occurrence, LOW lexical interpretation] Early longer descriptions include A·D=𗂱𘒳, B·𗢬=𗟎𗢬, and C·𗌈·D=𘍂𗌈𘒳. These may be full names, descriptions, synonyms, or erroneous spellings that later become abbreviated. Exact equivalence to later singleton labels is not proved.
• [LOW] 𗘗 and 𗌈 may be modifiers or components of item names; their standalone meanings cannot be recovered. X is not safely interpretable as “D-prime,” a shade, or any specific color.

Code proposals and reasoning
• 𗲅 [MEDIUM]: code/submission/submit construction. 𗒳𗲅𗉞 + digits ≈ “I submit/propose [code].” Exact distinction between noun “code” and verb “submit” is uncertain.
• 𗹫𗲅 [MEDIUM]: try/propose a code, often immediately preceding task feedback.
• 𗠄 [MEDIUM]: answer/propose, another construction accompanying candidate codes.
• 𗷤 [MEDIUM]: guess/think/hypothesize. It introduces tentative mappings as well as candidate codes.
• 𗭵 [LOW–MEDIUM]: only/just, often restricting the known information.
• 𗍽 [LOW]: combine/derive/collect or introduce a proposed table.
• 𘂲𗂺 [MEDIUM]: not correct/invalid/does not work, following previously attempted codes; 𗂺 may mean correct/valid.
• 𗜀 [LOW]: possible alternative first-person plural “we,” replacing 𗒳 in message 174.
• 𘖽 [LOW]: a relation between numbers, possibly change/to/arrow; occurs only as 1𘖽2 and 9𘖽6 in message 157.
• [LOW] Remaining rare glyphs/groups (including 𘂮𗕴, 𗼛, 𗭯, 𗷴, 𘊌, 𗸽𗋟, 𗳃, 𘖸, 𗡒, 𘉝) are not securely decoded. Their recognizable surrounding content is translated below.
