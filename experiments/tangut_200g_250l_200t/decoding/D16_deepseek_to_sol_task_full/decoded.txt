3. THE TASK

The public briefing describes a three-stage escape room: combine symbol positions and base digits to open a cabinet, operate power controls in a charge-feasible order, then combine original bases with new offsets to unlock the exit. The supplied internals give cabinet2748, power DRAIN→CRANK→SIGNAL→BOOT, and exit9796. Those are briefing facts, not decoded accomplishments.

The actual log covers only the cabinet stage. Its content fits distribution of the initial clues: agent4 repeatedly sends SUN2; agent2 WAVE8; agent0 a MOON-like compound7; agent6 T4; agent3 position1 SUN; agent5 position2 a MOON-like compound; agent1 position3 T/TS; agent7 position4 WAVE. These owner-specific patterns provide genuine lexical anchors.

They repeatedly exchange known and missing information, propose layouts, and try codes. They do not reach a stable common interpretation. Particularly damaging confusions include SUN being summarized as1 rather than2, MOON-like labels being assigned2, and the MOON7 compound being shortened to bare T7 and apparently confused with STAR. Even position order is sometimes changed: examples include SUN,T,M,WAVE and SUN,M,WAVE,T. Some agents continue marking previously announced information unknown. These observations support a communication failure, although exact causes—language ambiguity, improvised vocabulary, mistaken parsing, or ordinary agent errors—cannot all be separated.

The visible status never advances beyond stage0 and ends at32 invalid attempts. Several messages explicitly describe1248 as failed. There is no decoded evidence of successful cabinet opening, power actions, battery reasoning, new offsets, exit entry, or escape. Thus the log fits an unsuccessful attempt at the described task, not the worked solution. No final successful conclusion is present; the last visible guess is1728 followed by another rejection.

A. Independent clue anchors. The short message2 by the SUN-base owner is 𗂱𗉞𘀝. Agent2 repeatedly uses 𗟎𗉞𗽕. Agent0 repeatedly uses a distinct compound followed by 𗉞𘝚. Agent6 repeatedly asserts 𘒳𗉞𗨥. D.
