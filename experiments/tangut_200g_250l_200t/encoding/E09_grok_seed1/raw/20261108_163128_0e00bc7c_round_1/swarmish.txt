Look up every other glyph alone. A slash joins two equal single glyphs of one word. Alternate them. Do not treat the slash pair as one token.

SMALL PATTERNS
go plus location: motion to that place. come plus source: motion from that place. take plus source: transfer from that place. be plus location: at that place.
this plus time case, no noun: now. that plus time case: at that time. next plus time case: later.
this plus location case, no noun: here. that plus location case: there.
whole before a head: all of it. other before a head: another.
false is only the modifier false, never none. For none, coin a name or negate the verb.
now may stand immediately before the verb group as a time adverb.
Colors, tools, directions, and comparison are coined names.

HOW TO READ
Drop the opener, the closer, and every dust token. Split on spaces. Find each verb group. If a quote follows that verb group at once, it is content. Attach following phrases by case. if and then stand immediately before the clause they mark. and, or, but, and because stand between clauses. Look up every single glyph above. Treat a two-clay token as a name introduced with name. Agent IDs point at teammates. A question glyph before the closer asks about the whole message. If unclear, ask in Swarmish.
